Jul 2025 — Jun 2026SEDS UNLV · IREC 2026

Tachyon — recovery subsystem lead

Redundant dual-deploy recovery system for UNLV's IREC 2026 entry, built to the DTEG requirements. Led an 8+ member team through design, testing, integration, and documentation, working across avionics, structures, and propulsion.

The avionics sled carries two independent dual-deploy altimeters and a Featherweight GPS tracker, printed in ABS-GF for stiffness and heat resistance during summer launches. A MATLAB shock-load calculator predicts parachute deployment loads and drives the structural design; aluminum bulkheads were validated with ANSYS static FEA.

Aramis — recovery subsystem engineer

Redesigned and 3D printed the recovery avionics sled, integrating an SRAD Eggtimer Quasar with a COTS Altus Metrum TeleMetrum for independent apogee and main deployment circuits plus live GPS telemetry.

Built an Excel black-powder charge calculator that predicted ejection forces within ±5% of ground-test results, handled avionics integration and soldering, and ran OpenRocket flight simulations. Aramis flew a nominal test flight, then a flawless competition flight.

Oct 2024 — Mar 2025Tripoli Level 1 certification

Vulcan — Level 1 high-power rocket

Designed, built, and flew a 50-inch high-power rocket to earn Tripoli Rocketry Association Level 1 certification. Simulated in OpenRocket for stability margin, motor selection, apogee, and deployment timing before any parts were cut.

Fins were drafted in AutoCAD, laser-cut from plywood, and epoxied to the airframe. Launched March 15, 2025 at a Tripoli-certified event with a nominal flight and negligible damage on recovery.
